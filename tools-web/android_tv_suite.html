Android TV Web Suite

Universal remote, app launcher, live serial console & TV channel manager for ESP32 ADB.

Help & Privacy Information
Local-first & 100% Private

All channel edits, reordering, and configurations stay strictly on this PC and are never uploaded to any server or cloud. Commands are sent exclusively to your connected ESP32 via USB Web Serial.

Direct TV Channel Tuning

Click the green play button on any channel in the list (or double-click the row) to tune your Android TV tuner directly to that channel number.

Authentic TV Remote Sidebar

The collapsible right sidebar matches your physical TV remote control. All buttons transmit standard Android keycodes via the ESP32 ADB bridge.

App Management

Discover installed Android packages on your TV with Query Apps, and launch or force-stop them directly.
