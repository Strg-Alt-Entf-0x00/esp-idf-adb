Android TV ADB Studio · Reference & Setup

1. Android TV Preparation: Enable Developer Options on your TV (click "Build number" 7 times) and turn on Network Debugging (ADB over TCP/IP).

2. ESP32 Connection: Connect your ESP32 board via USB to your PC and click the serial connect icon in the top-right header (115200 Baud). Configure Wi-Fi in the Wi-Fi Setup tab if required.

3. Connect TV: Enter the target IP address and click the link icon. On first connection, accept the USB/Network debugging authorization prompt on your TV screen.

4. Keyboard Navigation: Arrow keys for D-Pad, Enter for OK, Esc for Back, H for Home, M for Menu, Space for Play/Pause, +/- for Volume.
